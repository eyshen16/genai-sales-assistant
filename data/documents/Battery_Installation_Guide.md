# HomeCell Battery Installation and Commissioning Guide

## 1. Purpose

This document provides installation and commissioning guidance for HomeCell residential battery systems used with approved VoltEdge hybrid inverters.

Product compatibility must be verified against the current approved compatibility data before installation.

## 2. Supported Products

This guide applies to:

- HomeCell 10
- HomeCell 15

Compatibility with individual inverter models and required inverter firmware versions is maintained separately in the approved product compatibility data.

## 3. Installation Requirements

HomeCell batteries must be installed by a qualified electrical professional in accordance with applicable local electrical regulations and VoltEdge installation instructions.

The installation location must:

- be protected from direct weather exposure;
- provide adequate ventilation;
- remain within the operating temperature range specified for the product;
- provide sufficient clearance for installation and service access.

## 4. Retrofit Installations

When a HomeCell battery is added to an existing VoltEdge hybrid inverter installation, the installer must verify the inverter firmware version before battery commissioning.

If a firmware update is required by the approved compatibility data, the inverter must be updated before the HomeCell battery is commissioned.

The existing installation should also be checked for compliance with current wiring and metering requirements.

## 5. Commissioning Sequence

For retrofit installations, the recommended sequence is:

1. Verify product compatibility.
2. Verify and, where required, update inverter firmware.
3. Install and connect the HomeCell battery.
4. Establish communication between battery and inverter.
5. Complete battery commissioning.
6. Verify correct system status in the monitoring interface.

The system must not be handed over to the customer if communication errors or unresolved commissioning warnings remain active.

## 6. System Integration

The HomeCell battery can operate as part of a VoltEdge residential energy system.

Advanced energy-management functions may require additional system components. Requirements for coordinated operation with EV charging and household energy optimization are described in the relevant EnergyHub and EV charger integration documentation.

## 7. Retrofit Limitations

Existing third-party equipment may affect the availability of advanced energy-management functions.

Where the installation includes unsupported third-party control equipment or a configuration not covered by approved VoltEdge documentation, the installer should request technical review before commissioning the complete system.

## 8. Warranty and Compliance

Installation and commissioning must follow current approved VoltEdge instructions.

Warranty conditions are governed by the applicable regional warranty policy. This guide does not override warranty terms.
