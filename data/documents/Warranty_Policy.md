# VoltEdge Residential Energy Warranty Policy - Germany

## 1. Scope

This policy defines the prototype warranty-governance rules for approved VoltEdge residential energy products and related system components in the Germany prototype corpus.

This policy is authoritative for warranty-eligibility questions, exclusions, and cases requiring further review. It is not the source of truth for product compatibility, firmware requirements, installation procedures, or system-integration specifications.

## 2. Warranty Eligibility

Warranty eligibility depends on whether the affected product or system component was installed, commissioned, operated, and maintained in accordance with current approved VoltEdge documentation.

Warranty coverage must not be assumed solely because a product is present in a VoltEdge installation. Eligibility depends on the documented configuration and the circumstances of the reported issue.

## 3. Approved Installation and Commissioning Requirements

For warranty eligibility, installation and commissioning must follow the current approved VoltEdge installation, commissioning, and integration guidance that applies to the affected product or system.

Where a claim depends on whether installation or commissioning was performed correctly, the relevant approved product or system guide must be reviewed. This policy does not replace those technical documents, but failure to follow them may affect warranty eligibility.

## 4. Unauthorized Modifications

Unauthorized modifications may affect warranty eligibility.

Changes to product wiring, device controls, communication paths, protection settings, enclosure integrity, or other implementation details that are not covered by approved VoltEdge documentation must not be assumed to remain under warranty.

Where a failure may be related to an unauthorized modification, warranty coverage must not be confirmed without authoritative review.

## 5. Third-Party Equipment and Unsupported Integrations

Unsupported third-party equipment, unapproved control integrations, or unvalidated system architectures may affect warranty eligibility.

If a claim involves third-party devices, third-party control logic, unsupported metering arrangements, or other integrations not explicitly covered by approved VoltEdge documentation, warranty applicability must not be assumed to be covered without authoritative review.

The presence of third-party equipment does not automatically void warranty coverage for every issue, but it prevents a blanket assumption of coverage where the third-party configuration may be relevant to the reported failure.

## 6. Exclusions

This prototype policy treats the following as common reasons that warranty coverage may be excluded or limited:

- failure to follow current approved VoltEdge installation or commissioning instructions;
- unauthorized modifications;
- unsupported third-party integrations or control configurations where they may be relevant to the reported issue;
- operation outside documented intended use;
- unresolved conditions that require technical review before eligibility can be determined.

## 7. Technical Review and Escalation

Where warranty applicability cannot be determined from the current approved warranty policy and the relevant approved technical documentation, the case must be escalated for authoritative review.

Escalation is required when:

- the installation includes unsupported or unclear third-party integrations;
- unauthorized modification is suspected;
- the available evidence is incomplete or conflicting;
- the relationship between the reported failure and the documented configuration cannot be determined from approved sources.

In such cases, the system should abstain from asserting that the configuration is covered and direct the case to the appropriate business or technical owner for review.
